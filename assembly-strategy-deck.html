Action Plan

The First 30 Days

Before you build the community, you must build the container. These 15 steps across four weeks transform the strategy into a living, breathing reality in Ogbomoso.

Week 1
Foundations & Recruitment
1
Approve the Budget
Review the ₦2M–₦5M budget allocation with The Assembly leadership. Get written sign-off on Phase 1 spend, including server hardware procurement and initial operating costs.
Leadership
2
Order Server Hardware
Place orders for the AI Lab server: AMD Ryzen 9/Intel i9 PC, NVIDIA RTX 4060 or 3080 GPU, 64GB RAM, NVMe SSD + HDD, APC UPS, and gigabit network switch. Budget: ₦815K–₦1.2M.
Technical
3
Set Up Community Channels
Create the Discord server (with channels: #general, #projects, #resources, #jobs, #ai-lab), WhatsApp announcement group, and GitHub organisation for The Assembly Ogbomoso.
Community
4
Begin Branding & Identity Work
Commission or create visual assets: community logo, event templates, social media headers, and a short community one-pager. Align with The Assembly's existing brand identity.
Leadership
5
Personally Invite 20 Developers
Make a handwritten list of the 20 most promising developers you know in Ogbomoso — students, self-taught, professionals. Call or meet each one personally. These are your founding 20. Do not mass-blast at this stage.
Community
Week 2
Infrastructure & Partnerships
6
Configure the AI Lab Server
Set up Ubuntu Server on the AI Lab machine. Install Ollama and pull initial models: DeepSeek Coder 6.7B, Llama 3.1 8B, CodeLlama 13B, and Mistral 7B. Test inference locally and confirm Starlink + UPS are working.
Technical
7
Write the Continue.dev Setup Guide
Create a step-by-step guide for members to install the Continue.dev VSCode extension and point it to the local Ollama server IP. Include screenshots, troubleshooting tips, and model selection guidance.
Technical
8
Publish the Membership Landing Page
Launch a simple, well-designed landing page (theassembly.city or subdomain) describing the community, the three membership tiers with pricing, and a sign-up form or waitlist link.
Launch
9
Brief OSCA and GDG Leads
Schedule meetings with the OSCA and GDG chapter leads in Ogbomoso. Share the community strategy, confirm the partnership model, align on co-hosting responsibilities, and agree on the first joint event date.
Community
Week 3
Launch Event Preparation
10
Set Launch Event Date & Create Event Page
Choose a date for the Community Launch Meetup (target: end of Month 1 or early Month 2). Create an event page on Eventbrite or a custom page. Set registration to free and begin promoting across channels.
Launch
11
Draft the Community Covenant
Write the Founding Covenant as a beautifully formatted document: 5 principles — excellence as worship, lifting each other, solving real problems, staying humble, and being redemptive. This will be signed or pledged at the launch event.
Community
12
Plan the Launch Event Programme
Design a 2–3 hour programme: welcome and vision talk, AI Lab demo, community structure overview, Covenant reading, open Q&A, and informal networking. Assign roles to founding members and partners. Prepare the AI Lab demo script.
Launch
Week 4
Launch & Review
13
Host the Community Launch Meetup
Execute the launch event. Introduce The Assembly developer community to Ogbomoso. Demo the AI Lab live. Invite attendees to sign the Community Covenant. Capture video and photos for documentation and social proof.
Launch
14
Send the First Newsletter
Within 48 hours of the launch, send the first community newsletter: recap of the launch, AI Lab access instructions, Month 2 events preview, curated learning resources, and how to upgrade to Core or Builder membership.
Community
15
Review Weeks 1–4 and Set Month 2 Targets
Hold an internal retrospective: what worked, what didn't, how many members joined, what the energy is like. Set concrete Month 2 targets (member count, first paid members, first workshop date) and assign owners to each.
Leadership
"The most important tool is not Ollama. It's the first 20 developers you personally invite."
Start there. Everything else follows.
